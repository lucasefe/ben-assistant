Privacy policy

Ben Kenobi is a private application used only by its owner. It requests access to the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) solely to perform tasks the owner asks for.

  • Data accessed through Google APIs is used only for the owner's own personal use and only on the owner's own server.
  • No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party, and none is used for advertising.
  • No data from other people is collected; the app has no other users.
  • The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: contact the repository owner through GitHub.
